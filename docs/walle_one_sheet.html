Pi-5 person-following rover

WALL‑E Mini

A ~40 kg tracked rover that follows you, dodges obstacles, and takes a phone or GPS route — with RC always holding the kill switch. Every autonomy layer sits under a hardware safety chain.

01

Autonomy

follow · avoid · navigate · retrace

Follow-Me person tracking4-layer

Locks onto one person and holds a 1.5 m follow distance. A layered pipeline keeps the lock honest:

  • Detection filter — rejects edge slivers, thin boxes & short ground blobs (bbox/width/implied-height gates)
  • Sticky target lock — 1.5 s switch-grace + higher 0.65 acquire bar; won't drop you for a closer, lower-confidence blob
  • Lateral PID steering with edge-boost gain to fight targets drifting to the frame edge
  • Depth-based speed with dead-zone & accel cap; open-loop fallback

Engage by RC (ch4), a hand gesture (3‑4‑3 fingers; open palm stops), or the dashboard — but only when a person is already in view.

Obstacle avoidancedepth tiers

Reads a depth corridor ahead and gates forward motion only (camera can't see behind):

  • Slow below 1.5 m, hard stop below 0.4 m
  • Person-stop tier — YOLO person/animal inside 0.8 m forces distance → 0
  • Stale-depth fail-safe — stops autonomy if depth data ages past 0.5 s

Waypoint / RTK-GPS navALIGN·DRIVE·ARRIVE

Three-state machine: pivot until heading error < 12°, drive with PID steering, arrive within 1 m then advance. Requires RTK-fixed (quality ≥ 4); stale GPS >3 s halts motion.

Trail pursuitpure-pursuit

Drops GPS/odometry breadcrumbs and follows the path you walked — not just your live position — so it rounds corners after losing line-of-sight. Adaptive lookahead, Savitzky-Golay smoothing, and curvature-based slowdown in tight turns.

02

Teleop & Control

RC boss · phone PWA · dashboard

RC authority modelboss

A 5-channel RC receiver is always in charge. ch3 arms/disarms, ch5 latches an emergency stop. The loop force-neutrals the motors unless RC keeps it armed — every autonomy & teleop path rides under this gate.

Phone /drive PWAfail-safe

Installable phone driver, fail-safe by construction — the robot stops if wifi drops, the tab sleeps, or a thread dies:

  • Server-side 250 ms deadman — a watchdog thread is the sole motor writer
  • Arm ceremony — hold-to-arm ≥500 ms + RC-armed
  • Latched e-stop from any client & single-driver lock
  • Speed caps 30 / 60 / 100 % + optional live camera over WS

Web dashboard:8080

Flask app on port 8080: live RGB + depth camera, SSE telemetry, browsable recordings, plus a live Follow-Me radar, pure-pursuit trail visualizer & RTK diagnostics — all served straight off the Pi.

Bluetooth overrideSPP

An external SPP service writes a fresh-within-600 ms command file the main loop honors as a manual override — still gated by RC arm state.

03

Perception

depth · detection · heading

Depth corridor

OAK-D Lite stereo depth sampled in a tuned ROI (width, height, camera geometry all modeled). Valid-pixel % threshold ignores sparse/noisy corridors; USB-drop self-heal rebuilds the pipeline without a service restart.

YOLOv8n pipelinedepthai v3

80-class COCO detection via a NeuralNetwork node + host-side NMS — the stock Luxonis detection nodes silently return zero dets with ultralytics blobs. Host-side IoU + Kalman tracklets give each person a stable track id across brief occlusions.

IMU heading

Onboard BMI270 (gyro + accel, no magnetometer) integrated to a startup-relative heading, feeding a PID heading-hold that keeps straight lines straight over bumps. External I2C IMU auto-preferred if present; GPS course-over-ground alignment re-zeros drift.

04

Layered safety

hardware authority down to graceful shutdown
RC
ch3 arm / ch5 e-stop are authoritative. ch5 latches emergency + shutdown; a >1 s RC-stale watchdog disarms. Final gate: not-armed → motors neutral.
SEE
Obstacle tiers slow then stop on depth; a person/animal inside 0.8 m forces a full stop; stale depth (>0.5 s) halts autonomy.
CHG
Charger inhibit — BMS-detected charging refuses drive commands, debounced (≥2 A over consecutive polls) so a regen blip can't false-trip; fail-open after 30 s of BLE silence.
PACK
BMS FET / pack-low guard — discharge-FET grace at arm, then force-neutral if it stays off; VESC low-voltage watchdog triggers graceful OS shutdown at 39 V (13S @ 3.0 V/cell) held 10 s, latched.
HAND
Phone deadman — 250 ms server-side watchdog, latched e-stop from any client, single-driver lock, speed caps.
UPS
Graceful shutdown chain — UPSPlus HAT keeps the Pi alive through pack cutoff; voltage-keyed AC detection (debounced) with auto-recovery on power return.
05

Power

13S pack · BMS · UPS chain

13S main pack

13-series Li-ion drives both VESCs. VESC STATUS frames carry pack voltage; a sustained-low watchdog at 39 V triggers a clean shutdown before cells are stressed.

Daly BMSBLE

Daly SPIM08HP over Bluetooth polls SOC, per-cell voltages, temp & MOSFET status every 8 s. Auto-reconnects; failures never block the control loop. Drives charger-inhibit & pack-low logic.

UPSPlus HAT18650

18650-backed UPS powers the Pi so main-pack cutoff never yanks power mid-write. Voltage-keyed AC detection + debounce drives the graceful-shutdown countdown, with auto-recovery when power returns.

06

Telemetry & Data

stream · record · replay

Live SSE telemetry

The dashboard streams live state over Server-Sent Events — mode, arm/e-stop, follow-me target, obstacle distance, BMS & VESC health.

Flight recorderJSONL

Structured ~10 Hz telemetry log, segmented per arm/disarm cycle for easy session analysis. Arm-gated: full rate while armed, a 5 s heartbeat while idle (~99 % less idle disk I/O) — but ARM/DISARM/e-stop & mode flips log instantly.

MCAP recordingsactivity-triggered

Records when Follow-Me or obstacle avoidance fires: on-device H.265 video (near-zero CPU) plus MCAP annotated snapshots + telemetry, with a 2 s pre-buffer and auto storage pruning.

07

Dev & Tooling

calibrate · tune · bench-test

Calibration wizard/calibrate

Browser-guided relay-feedback auto-tune (Ziegler-Nichols) for heading PID, Follow-Me steering & straight-line bias — with a one-click write tuned gains back into config.py. Plus a fleet of CLI calibration harnesses.

Autotune & replay

Offline tools score & Bayesian-optimize Follow-Me steering gains from recorded runs, and replay logged sessions (JSONL or MCAP) through the controller for regression analysis.

Detect-only bench modesafe

Runs the UPS detection chain end-to-end without firing any shutdown action — verify AC-loss detection on the bench without risking a real power-down.

~580-test suite

~580 unit tests across 35 files cover the safety state machine, follow-me targeting, obstacle math, VESC/BMS/UPS logic, teleop deadman & more.
